The Guidance and Counseling Center (GCC) is a unit under the Office of Student Affairs and Services Division (OSAS) that provides various programs and activities to support the holistic development of students. The GCC aims to help students cope with their academic, personal, social, and career concerns through counseling, testing, information dissemination, consultation, and referral services.
GUIDANCE AND COUNSELING SERVICE
I. INDIVIDUAL INVENTORY SERVICE
This service involves the systematic collection, evaluation, and interpretation of comprehensive information about students.
II. INFORMATION SERVICE
This service involves the systematic collection and distribution of information through various methods and programs to support students in their personal, social, academic, emotional, occupational, and spiritual development.
III. COUNSELING SERVICE
This service gives students a chance to understand themselves better, grow as individuals, and solve problems through one-on-one or group counseling.
IV. TESTING SERVICE
Individuals are alike as well as different. Psychological testing is one of the tools for assessment used to provide a description of an individual’s abilities, interests, aptitudes, preferences, and characteristics.
V. FOLLOW-UP SERVICE
This service aims to assess the effectiveness of interventions, monitor student progress, and provide additional assistance as needed to promote academic success, emotional well-being, and personal development.
VI. PREVENTION AND WELLNESS SERVICE
The Prevention and Wellness program supports TPC bonafide students in their personal efforts to maintain good health and achieve social balance.
VII. CAREER DEVELOPMENT SERVICE
The Career Development Service is designed to guide students in identifying their interests, developing career goals, and preparing for their professional journey.
VIII. PLACEMENT SERVICE
The Placement Service is designed to assist students in making informed decisions about their academic journey.
IX. REFERRAL SERVICES
This refers to connecting with agencies, organizations, or individuals that can offer more effective support in helping the counselee address their issues and achieve their full potential.
X. CONSULTATION SERVICE
The Consultation Service highlights the importance of seeking the opinions and decisions of others to positively impact a program.
OTHER SERVICES
1.Student Peer Support Program (PEER FACILITATORS CIRCLE)
The TPC Wellness Hub is the official name of the Peer Facilitators’ Circle of Talibon Polytechnic College. They are a group of TPC students who have finished the required workshops and training to become certified members of the Peer Facilitators’ Circle. They are carefully selected and officially recognized by the Guidance and Counseling Center, meeting the qualifications set by the Student Development Office.
The group actively takes part in student activities and programs organized by the Guidance and Counseling Center. They also help in counseling sessions and student lectures under the supervision of Guidance Counselors and Staff.
