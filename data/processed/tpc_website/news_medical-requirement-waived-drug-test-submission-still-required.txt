Attention, Talibon Polytechnic College students!
Please be informed that the Medical Requirement is temporarily waived during enrollment. However, all enrolled students are still required to submit their Drug Test Result on or before July 2026 as part of the enrollment compliance requirements.
✅ Medical Requirement – Waived during enrollment
✅ Drug Test Result – Still required
📅 Deadline for submission: July 2026
We encourage everyone to comply within the given period to avoid delays in the processing of student records.
