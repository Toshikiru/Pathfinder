Bachelor of Science in Information Systems
The Bachelor of Science in Information Systems (BSIS) at Talibon Polytechnic College is a 4-year program focusing on designing and implementing computer-based information systems and ICT solutions to meet business needs. It equips students with skills in systems development, business process analysis, and ICT implementation. Graduates can work as Systems Analysts, IT Project Managers, or Information Security Specialists.

BSIS Department || Community Outreach " Basic Computer Literacy Skills Program" with Zamora, Talibon, Bohol
For the past two days, the BSIS Department conducted the Basic Computer Literacy Skills Program, a community outreach initiative in partnership with the Barangay Officials of Zamora, Talibon, Bohol.
The two-day activity featured a series of educational sessions designed to help the officials deepen their understanding of computer literacy, documentation, and digital communication—essential skills that can make their daily responsibilities more efficient and effective.
With the support of the TPC Administration, the BSIS Faculty, and a few selected BSIS student officers, the team successfully facilitated the training and workshop, ensuring that the participants gained confidence and familiarity in using computers.
We extend our heartfelt appreciation to the TPC Administration, the BSIS Faculty, the dedicated student officers, and the Barangay Officials of Barangay Zamora for their cooperation, enthusiasm, and commitment that made this program meaningful and impactful.
