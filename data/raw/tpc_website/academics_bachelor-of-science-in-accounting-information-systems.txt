Bachelor of Science in Accounting Information Systems
The Bachelor of Science in Accounting Information Systems (BSAIS) at Talibon Polytechnic College is a 4-year program that combines accounting principles with information systems and technology. It focuses on designing, implementing, and managing accounting systems, ensuring accurate financial data management and compliance.
