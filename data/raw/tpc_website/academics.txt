BECED
Bachelor in Early Childhood Education
Early Childhood Education
The Bachelor in Early Childhood Education (BECE) at Talibon Polytechnic College is a 4-year program that prepares students to teach and care for young children (0-8 years old). It focuses on child development, curriculum design, and teaching strategies.

Bachelor of Science in Agriculture
Agriculture
The Bachelor of Science in Agriculture at Talibon Polytechnic College is a 4-year program that focuses on crop production, animal science, and agricultural management. It develops skills in farming, agribusiness, and sustainable agriculture.
View Program
BSAIS
Bachelor of Science in Accounting Information Systems
Accounting Information Systems
The Bachelor of Science in Accounting Information Systems (BSAIS) at Talibon Polytechnic College is a 4-year program that combines accounting principles with information systems and technology. It focuses on designing, implementing, and managing accounting systems, ensuring accurate financial data management and compliance.

BSC
Bachelor of Science in Criminology
Criminology
The Bachelor of Science in Criminology at Talibon Polytechnic College is a 4-year program that focuses on crime prevention, investigation, and correction. It develops skills in law enforcement, forensic science, and criminal justice.
View Program
BSIS
Bachelor of Science in Information Systems
Information Systems
The Bachelor of Science in Information Systems (BSIS) at Talibon Polytechnic College is a 4-year program focusing on designing and implementing computer-based information systems and ICT solutions to meet business needs. It equips students with skills in systems development, business process analysis, and ICT implementation. Graduates can work as Systems Analysts, IT Project Managers, or Information Security Specialists.
