Vision
The Office of the Registrar, guided by its mandate, aspires to be a department of professional excellence fostering students’ success by assisting them in achieving their academic goals with recognition from a student-centered environment.
Mission
Provide excellent academic support services with emphasis on the college mission by modelling the highest standards of the registrar profession in providing exemplary services to students, faculty, staff and other stakeholders while ensuring the utmost academic records integrity.
Goals
The Office of the Registrar aims to:
Perform the mandated functions of the college through an effective and efficient records management system.
Maintain, update, safeguard and preserve students’ academic records.
Provide accurate records/documents to clients and other stakeholders.

Services
The registrar's office exists to ensure the accurate management of student records, facilitate enrollment processes, and uphold academic integrity. It serves as a central hub for maintaining essential data, providing administrative support, and ensuring compliance with educational regulations. The Registrar’s Office Services includes:
Course Registration/Enrolment. Registering/enrolling for subjects each semester, including adding, dropping, and changing of subjects.
Record Keeping. This office is responsible for maintaining comprehensive student records, which include transcripts, grades, and personal information.
Transfer Credit Evaluation. Evaluating and processing transfer credits from other institutions to determine their equivalency to courses at the registrar's institution.
Changing/Adding/ Dropping of Subjects. Processing requests from students to change/add/drop subjects.
Form 137 Requests to SHS. Requesting permanent records that contains a comprehensive record of a student's academic performance and achievements throughout their Senior High schooling
Enrollment Verification. Providing verification of a student's enrollment status for various purposes.
ID Validation. ID validation ensures the authenticity and accuracy of identification documents during the semester
Leave of Absence. Processing requests from students for temporary leaves of absence from their academic studies.
Degree Verification. Providing verification of degrees earned by current or former students to third parties such as employers or other educational institutions.
Release and Process Transcripts / Certifications. Release official transcripts or certifications for academic, employment and other purposes.
Graduation Application. Applying for graduation and completing necessary paperwork to ensure eligibility for graduation.
Academic Calendar. Providing information on important academic dates, such as registration periods, holidays, and exam schedules.
Graduation Clearance Reviewing students' academic records to ensure all requirements for graduation have been met and providing clearance for graduation.
Student's Evaluation/Advising. Providing guidance and assistance to students regarding their academic progress, course selection, and degree requirements.
Released of Diploma. Issuing diplomas to graduates and to students who have lost or damaged their original diploma (upon request).
Grade Processing. Overseeing the submission and processing of grades by faculty at the end of each semester.
HEMIS Submission. Submit
Higher Education Management Information System (HEMIS)
Data to CHED Regional Office 7 every academic year.
Compliance and Reporting: The registrar's office guarantees adherence to legal standards and prepares necessary reports for accreditation and governmental agencies.
Academic Policy Implementation: This office enforces academic policies concerning grading, academic standing, and appeals, ensuring a consistent application across the institution.
