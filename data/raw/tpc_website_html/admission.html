Enrollment & Requirements

Learn the enrollment steps and prepare the needed documents to become a student of Talibon Polytechnic College.

View Requirements Enrollment Process

Admission Requirements

Bring original copies and photocopies as applicable.

For Freshmen

  • Grade 12 Report Card/Form 137 with at least 2nd quarter grade (Original)
  • PSA Birth Certificate (Original)
  • 1 Long Brown Envelope
  • 1 Long White Folder
  • 2pcs 2x2 Picture (with name & course indicated at the back)
  • Entrance Exam Result
  • Good Moral Certificate

For Transferees

  • Transfer of Credentials (Hon. Dismissal & TOR) (Original)
  • PSA Birth Certificate (Original)
  • 1 Long Brown Envelope
  • 1 Long White Folder
  • 2pcs 2x2 Picture (with name & course indicated at the back)
  • Entrance Exam Result
  • Good Moral Certificate

Note

Requirements may vary depending on the program. Please confirm with the admissions office for the latest official list.

Enrollment Process

1

Prepare your requirements

Complete the documents listed above before visiting the office.

2

Submit documents to Admissions

Proceed to the admissions office and present your documents for screening.

3

Choose your program

Select the program that best fits your goals and career path.

4

Assessment and payment

Follow the cashier and assessment instructions as directed.

5

Confirm enrollment

Receive your confirmation slip and final enrollment instructions.
