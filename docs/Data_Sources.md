# Data Sources

Pathfinder's knowledge base has three categories of source data. All are documented
here in full so the provenance of every document in `/data/raw` is traceable.

## 1. Real data — scraped from tpc.edu.ph (10 documents)

Fetched 2026-08-24 from Talibon Polytechnic College's public website
(`https://www.tpc.edu.ph/`). Saved as raw HTML in `data/raw/tpc_website_html/`
and extracted to plain text in `data/raw/tpc_website/` by
[`src/01_extract_raw_text.py`](../src/01_extract_raw_text.py).

| Page | URL |
|---|---|
| Academics overview | `/academics` |
| BS Information Systems | `/academics/bachelor-of-science-in-information-systems` |
| Admission & enrollment | `/admission` |
| Registrar's Office | `/services/registrars-office` |
| Guidance & Counseling Center | `/services/guidance-and-counseling-center` |
| Contact | `/contact` |
| News (index + 3 articles) | `/news`, `/news/*` |

**Why these pages:** they cover the institutional-knowledge side of academic
advising — program descriptions, program leadership, admission requirements,
and which office to contact for what — that a real advising assistant needs
alongside curriculum data. Every page here is about BSIS or general TPC
institutional info a currently-enrolled BSIS student would need.

**Removed as out-of-scope (no longer in the corpus):** 6 pages describing
*other* TPC degree programs — BS Accounting Information Systems, BS
Agriculture, BS Criminology, BA English Language, BA Political Science, and
Bachelor in Early Childhood Education. These were originally scraped along
with everything else on the public site to help meet the capstone guide's
20–30 document minimum, not because they served BSIS advising. Once the
corpus had other real content to rely on for that minimum (the official
curriculum checklist, Section 2), keeping pages about unrelated degree
programs stopped being defensible, so they were dropped entirely rather than
kept as filler. (The news articles remain for the same reason they were
flagged before — not core advising content, but removing them too would
drop the corpus to 19 documents, under the guide's minimum; see the total
count below.)

**Known limitation:** TPC's public site is a marketing/informational site for
prospective students, not a student-facing academic portal. It never
published the curriculum checklist or the full student handbook online. The
checklist gap is now closed (see Section 2 below) via a physical copy
obtained directly; the full handbook (grading policy, academic standing
rules in narrative form) is still not accessible — a 52-page TPC Student
Handbook exists on Scribd but is preview-locked — so that content remains
simulated (Section 3).

## 2. Real data — official BSIS curriculum checklist (8 documents)

Transcribed from a printed, signed copy of Talibon Polytechnic College's
official BSIS curriculum checklist, effective A.Y. 2024–2025, obtained
directly by a team member (photographed pages of the physical document —
TPC does not publish this checklist online, per the limitation above).
Written into `data/raw/curriculum/01..08_*.txt` by
[`src/00_generate_curriculum_data.py`](../src/00_generate_curriculum_data.py),
each carrying a `[REAL DATA]` disclosure banner (not `[SIMULATED DATA]`) as
its first line.

**Basis:** CHED Memorandum Order No. 25, s. 2015 and CMO No. 20, s. 2013 —
the checklist's own header cites both as the standards it implements.
**Prepared by** Flordelis A. Turtoga, MAMT (Program Head, BSIS) — the same
name already present in the real scraped page
`tpc_website/academics_bachelor-of-science-in-information-systems.txt`,
which independently corroborates this is genuinely TPC's document.
**Recommending approval** by the VP for Academic Affairs and VP for
Administration; **approved by** the College President.

Every course code, title, unit count, lecture/lab hour split, and
prerequisite in these 8 files is real, transcribed as printed (8 terms:
Year 1–4, two semesters each — the checklist has no summer term). This
**replaces** the simulated 9-term curriculum used in Checkpoints 1–2, whose
course codes (`IS301`, `IS401`, etc.) were entirely invented; the real
codes (`CC1xx`, `IS1xx`, `BED1xx`, `CAP1xx`, etc.) look different and do not
overlap with the old simulated ones. One minor internal inconsistency in the
source document itself is worth flagging: Year 3, Second Semester's printed
per-course lecture hours sum to 12, but the document's own TOTAL row says
11 — transcribed as printed rather than silently corrected.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `curriculum/01..08_*.txt` | Term-by-term BSIS curriculum checklist (Year 1 Sem 1 → Year 4 Sem 2), 8 terms, real codes/titles/units/lecture-lab hours/prerequisites |

## 3. Simulated data — course descriptions & policy set (5 documents)

Generated by the same [`src/00_generate_curriculum_data.py`](../src/00_generate_curriculum_data.py)
into `data/raw/curriculum/course_descriptions_*.txt` (3 files) and
`data/raw/policies/` (2 files). Every file carries a `[SIMULATED DATA]`
disclosure banner as its first line.

**What is real vs. authored:** the course *codes and titles* referenced in
the description catalog are all real, taken from the checklist in Section
2. The *descriptive text* itself (what each course covers) is authored for
this project, since the official checklist has no accompanying course
catalog. The two policy documents' wording (grading scale, probation rules,
add/drop windows) is likewise authored, since TPC's full student handbook
isn't accessible — **except** the Capstone/Practicum prerequisite line in
`enrollment_prerequisites_and_academic_standing.txt`, which was corrected
this checkpoint to state the real prerequisite (`CAP 101` requires 3rd Year
Standing; `PRACTICUM` requires 4th Year Standing, per the real checklist)
instead of the earlier simulated version's invented specific-course
requirement.

**Why simulate instead of skip:** the project's whole premise —
course-planning and degree-progress advising — depends on grading/GWA and
academic-standing rules that aren't published anywhere accessible. Without
some version of this data there is no RAG use case to build for
degree-progress questions. Simulating it transparently was judged more
honest than fabricating it without disclosure.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `curriculum/course_descriptions_*.txt` | Course description catalog, 3 files by category, real codes/authored descriptions |
| `policies/grading_system_and_gwa_policy.txt` | Grading scale, GWA computation, Latin honors, INC rule |
| `policies/enrollment_prerequisites_and_academic_standing.txt` | Prerequisite enforcement, unit caps, probation/dismissal, capstone/practicum eligibility (real prereq terms), add/drop |

## Total document count: 23

10 real (scraped) + 8 real (official curriculum) + 5 simulated (descriptions
+ policies) = 23 documents, within the guide's 20–30 range.
