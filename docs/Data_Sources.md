# Data Sources

Pathfinder's knowledge base has two categories of source data. Both are documented
here in full so the provenance of every document in `/data/raw` is traceable.

## 1. Real data — scraped from tpc.edu.ph (16 documents)

Fetched 2026-08-24 from Talibon Polytechnic College's public website
(`https://www.tpc.edu.ph/`). Saved as raw HTML in `data/raw/tpc_website_html/`
and extracted to plain text in `data/raw/tpc_website/` by
[`src/01_extract_raw_text.py`](../src/01_extract_raw_text.py).

**Why these pages:** they cover the institutional-knowledge side of academic
advising — program descriptions, program leadership, admission requirements,
and which office to contact for what — that a real advising assistant needs
alongside curriculum data.

**Known limitation:** TPC's public site is a marketing/informational site for
prospective students, not a student-facing academic portal. It does not
publish the actual curriculum checklist (course codes, units, prerequisites)
or the full student handbook (grading policy, academic standing rules). A
52-page TPC Student Handbook exists on Scribd but is preview-locked and its
text could not be extracted. This gap is filled by the simulated dataset below.

## 2. Simulated data — CHED-CMO-based curriculum & policy set (14 documents)

Generated by [`src/00_generate_curriculum_data.py`](../src/00_generate_curriculum_data.py)
into `data/raw/curriculum/` (12 files) and `data/raw/policies/` (2 files).
Every file carries a `[SIMULATED DATA]` disclosure banner as its first line.

**Basis:** CHED Memorandum Order No. 25, s. 2015, *"Policies, Standards and
Guidelines for Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, Information
Systems, and Information Technology Programs"* — the actual national policy
the Commission on Higher Education uses to regulate BSIS curricula in the
Philippines. This is real, verifiable, and is why the simulated curriculum's
term structure, course categories (General Education / Common / Professional
/ Electives), and unit loads are realistic rather than invented from nothing.

**What is authored (not from CHED):** the specific course codes (`IS301`,
etc.), course descriptions, and the academic policy wording (grading scale,
probation rules, add/drop windows) are original content written for this
project, since TPC's own registrar prospectus is not publicly accessible.

**Why simulate instead of skip:** the project's whole premise —
course-planning and degree-progress advising — depends on exactly this data
(prerequisites, unit caps, GWA rules). Without it there is no RAG use case
to build. Simulating it transparently, on a real regulatory basis, was
judged more honest than either fabricating it without disclosure or
choosing a theme with no real advising content to ground.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `curriculum/01..09_*.txt` | Term-by-term BSIS curriculum checklist (Year 1 Sem 1 → Year 4 Sem 2), 9 terms |
| `curriculum/course_descriptions_*.txt` | Course description catalog, 3 files by category |
| `policies/grading_system_and_gwa_policy.txt` | Grading scale, GWA computation, Latin honors, INC rule |
| `policies/enrollment_prerequisites_and_academic_standing.txt` | Prerequisite enforcement, unit caps, probation/dismissal, capstone/practicum eligibility, add/drop |

## Total document count: 30

16 real + 14 simulated = 30 documents, exceeding the guide's 20–30 minimum.
